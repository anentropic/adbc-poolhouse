<objective>
Extend `AsyncConnection` with the one genuinely new bit of production machinery this phase adds:
a persistent `_reader_open` lifetime flag plus a two-tier entry guard. A live reader locks the
parent connection for its whole lifetime (Model B, D-29-08/09/10); foreign callers are rejected
with `ConnectionBusyError` while a reader is open, but the reader's own per-batch pulls are exempt
from the reader tier (reentrancy) via a `from_reader=True` keyword — while still taking the
per-call `_in_use` C-access tier.

Purpose: STREAM-06 forbids concurrent C access on a connection with a live Arrow stream; the
per-call `_in_use` flag alone reads False between pulls, leaving a gap a foreign op could exploit.
Output: `_connection.py` gains `_reader_open` + `from_reader` threading, with every existing call
site untouched.
</objective>
