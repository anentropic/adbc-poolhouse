## Cross-Phase Integration

Integration checker verdict: **WIRED — 0 blockers, 0 warnings.**

- All 6 new `AsyncCursor` methods (`fetch_record_batch`, `adbc_ingest`, `fetch_df`,
  `fetch_polars`, `adbc_prepare`, `adbc_execute_schema`) route through the shared
  `offload`/`cancellable_offload` chokepoint under the per-pool `CapacityLimiter`.
  No bare `to_thread`, no `import asyncio` outside `_offload.py`.

  streaming ones surface `AsyncRecordBatchReader` with `poison_on_cancel=False`.

  every new method; **basedpyright strict = 0 errors** (PKG-01).
- **PKG-03 import-lint** (`scan_async_package`) = 0 findings.
- Cancel/invalidate semantics are differentiated correctly: `adbc_ingest` poisons on
  abort (`on_abort=invalidate`); cursor streaming fires `adbc_cancel` + invalidates
  (`poison_on_cancel=True`); metadata + prepared-statement methods fire `adbc_cancel`
  once but do **not** poison the pool (`checkedout()==0` after cancel).
- The `_reader_open` lifetime lock composes consistently across Phases 29 and 34.
